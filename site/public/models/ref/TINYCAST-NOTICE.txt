TinyCast weights in GGUF form (tinycast.gguf) and their Rust port (crates/sky-infer/src/cast/tinycast.rs)

tinycast.gguf is a format conversion of the official TinyCast weights
(https://huggingface.co/raws-labs/tinycast, model.safetensors + config.json):
the same tensors, stored as GGUF by `sky-cast convert-tinycast`, with the
license, organization and source URL in its metadata. The shared feed-forward
weights are stored once. No weight was changed.
