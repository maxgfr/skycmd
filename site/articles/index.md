# Articles

Longer write-ups of the project's experiments, for a wide technical audience. Newest first.

- **[A 250 KB secrets verifier: rules propose, the model judges, and the gate still says no](/articles/a-250-kb-secrets-verifier)**, 16 November 2026. A tiny model that judges the candidates of a rule-based secret scanner: a first version that passed on its own generated data and failed on real files, then a second, trained and tested on real repositories, that beats every rule scanner tested and still misses its pre-registered bar by 0.027.
- **[A 3.7 MB tool router built on a MacBook: two failures, then a pass and its limits](/articles/a-3-7-mb-tool-router)**, 16 November 2026. A small model that picks an assistant’s tool: two failed versions, published as failures, a method written down before every test, and a third version that passes, without hiding what that pass does not prove.
